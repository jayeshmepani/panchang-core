# v11.4.1 — Gajachchhaya and Tripushkar Yoga Corrections

**Release Date:** October 2026

---

## Overview

This update corrects two auspicious-yoga timings. Festival dates, vrat dates, and the Vedic clocks from v11.4.0 stay as they are.

## Gajachchhaya Yoga

Gajachchhaya falls during Pitru Paksha, from sunrise to the next sunrise.

- On Krishna Trayodashi, the Sun is in Hasta and the Moon is in Magha.
- On Amavasya, the Sun and the Moon are both in Hasta.

The earlier Amavasya timing asked for the Moon in Magha. On a new-moon day the Moon stays with the Sun, so that pairing cannot happen. For Bhuj in 2026 the Amavasya window runs from the evening of 9 October through the evening of 10 October.

## Tripushkar Yoga

Tripushkar still needs Dwitiya, Saptami, or Dwadashi, and it still falls on Sunday, Tuesday, or Saturday.

The star used for the Phalguni portion is now Uttara Phalguni. Purva Phalguni was listed by mistake. The six stars are Krittika, Punarvasu, Uttara Phalguni, Vishakha, Uttara Ashadha, and Purva Bhadrapada.

Dwipushkar is unchanged: Mrigashira, Chitra, and Dhanishtha, on the same lunar days and weekdays.

## Left as they were

Sarvartha Siddhi, Maitreya (Tuesday with Ashwini in Aries, or Tuesday with Anuradha in Scorpio), and Panchaka Rahita are not part of this update.
