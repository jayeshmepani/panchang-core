# v11.3.0 — Panchaka Rahita Full-Day Muhurta Timeline & Precision Panchak Calculations

## Overview

Version 11.3.0 brings a major enhancement to electional astrology (Muhurta) with the introduction of the **Full-Day Panchaka Rahita Timeline** and refined, high-precision Panchak calculations based on classical Vedic astrological texts.

Panchaka Rahita (literally *"free from Panchaka defects"*) is one of the most essential principles in Vedic astrology for determining auspicious timings for significant life events—such as marriages, travel, Griha Pravesh (housewarming), starting new businesses, construction, and sacred ceremonies.

### 2. Classical Panchaka Classifications Explained

According to classical authorities like *Muhurta Chintamani*, when evaluating any moment for an auspicious undertaking, the formula:

$$\text{Panchaka} = (\text{Tithi} + \text{Weekday} + \text{Nakshatra} + \text{Lagna}) \pmod 9$$

is used to classify the period into one of the following nine states:

| Remainder | Panchak Type | Quality | Description & Traditional Guidance |
|:---:|:---|:---:|:---|
| **0** | **Nish-Panchaka** | **Auspicious** | Completely defect-free. Excellent for all auspicious ceremonies and beginnings. |
| **1** | **Mrityu Panchaka** | *Inauspicious* | Associated with danger and bodily harm; should be avoided for critical ventures. |
| **2** | **Agni Panchaka** | *Inauspicious* | Associated with fire hazards, accidents, and aggression; avoid starting construction or entering homes. |
| **3** | **Shubha Panchaka** | **Auspicious** | Highly beneficial and auspicious for ceremonies, purchases, and ventures. |
| **4** | **Raja Panchaka** | *Inauspicious* | Inauspicious for government matters, legal agreements, contracts, and new enterprises. |
| **5** | **Shubha Panchaka** | **Auspicious** | Auspicious and peaceful for all good deeds, journeys, and celebrations. |
| **6** | **Chora Panchaka** | *Inauspicious* | Associated with deceit, loss, theft, and financial instability; avoid starting travels or trade. |
| **7** | **Shubha Panchaka** | **Auspicious** | Auspicious and favorable for spiritual, domestic, and commercial activities. |
| **8** | **Roga Panchaka** | *Inauspicious* | Associated with disease, fatigue, and delays; avoid medical surgeries, convalescence starts, or major commitments. |

> **Key Rule of Thumb:** Periods falling under **Shubha Panchaka (Remainders 3, 5, 7)** or **Nish-Panchaka (Remainder 0)** are auspicious and recommended for important works. Periods under **Remainders 1, 2, 4, 6, and 8** carry Panchaka Dosha and are avoided for critical ceremonies.
